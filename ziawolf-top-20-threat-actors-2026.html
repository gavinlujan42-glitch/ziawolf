Wolf's-eye view

Nation-state actors pursue strategic access, intelligence or disruption. Criminal ecosystems pursue leverage and money. Private-sector offensive actors commercialize targeted surveillance. Their motivations differ, but their attack surfaces overlap: identity, vulnerable edge infrastructure, people, suppliers, mobile devices and trusted relationships.

New Mexico priorities

Protect identity with phishing-resistant MFA; harden help-desk verification; continuously inventory exposed systems; shorten patch latency; segment IT/OT; preserve manual operations for essential services; test immutable recovery; validate telecom/vendor trust; strengthen remote-worker and contractor proofing; and hunt behaviors rather than relying on actor labels.
