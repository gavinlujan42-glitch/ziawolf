# The ZiaWolf Human-Governed Agentic Architecture

**Doctrine version: 2026-09-12**

**AI supplies velocity. People retain authority.**

ZiaWolf agents are specialized production capacity inside a human-owned governance system. Every consequential operation follows the chain:

**Client → Contract → Scope → Policy → Identity → Agent → Evidence → Human Authority → Client**

## Five levels of authority
- **0 Observe:** gather evidence.
- **1 Recommend:** propose action.
- **2 Generate:** create code/configuration/change artifacts without execution.
- **3 Human-approved execution:** an authorized person approves the specific change.
- **4 Bounded autonomy:** only pre-authorized, reversible, low-blast-radius actions with full evidence and rollback.

## Factory implication
Every agent has a named human authority. Every consequential release has an accountable gate. RAG context informs decisions but never grants authority. Security, provenance, accessibility, recovery, auditability and rollback are part of the product, not paperwork added afterward.

## Picarus
Picarus Sentinel is the defensive specialist of the factory. It can see deeply and respond quickly, but cannot silently enlarge its own privileges. Missing recovery proof raises risk. Telemetry drives recommendations. Automated remediation is bounded by policy and authorization tier.

## Commercial discipline
Sell outcomes through advisory hours, architecture sprints, assessments, managed services and fixed deliverables. Meter infrastructure/model consumption separately or include it transparently. Never equate machine processing time with human professional-service labor.

**Enterprise architecture without enterprise overhead. Human-accountable AI, evidence everywhere, authority unmistakably human.**
